JIMNAYA · CUSTOMER INFORMATION

Privacy notice

Publication note: This is a practical website privacy notice template. Jimnaya must review it against its actual business processes, retention practices and applicable data-protection obligations before launch. Replace the contact placeholders in the hosting configuration.

Jimnaya Trusted Global Agency (“Jimnaya”, “we”, “us”) respects the privacy of people who contact us through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit a travel enquiry, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, requested service, destination, preferred travel date, traveller details, indicative budget and the message you provide.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss travel arrangements.
  • To prepare or follow up on a quotation or booking request.
  • To maintain appropriate business records and protect the website against misuse.

How information is handled

Enquiries are stored in Jimnaya’s website data store and are accessible to authorised personnel through a password-protected administration area. If email alerts are configured, enquiry details are also sent to the business notification address. Do not include passport numbers, payment-card details, medical details or other sensitive information in the enquiry form.

Sharing

If you ask us to arrange travel, relevant information may need to be shared with airlines, accommodation providers, tour operators or other service providers involved in your requested arrangements. Information may also be disclosed where required by law. Jimnaya should ensure that any actual sharing practices are accurately described in this notice.

Retention and security

Jimnaya should retain enquiry information only for as long as it is needed for the purpose collected, legitimate business records and applicable legal requirements, and then securely delete it. Access controls, HTTPS, secure hosting, backups and restricted staff access should be maintained. No website can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and requests

You may contact Jimnaya to ask about personal information submitted through this website or to request correction or deletion where applicable. Contact the business email address published on the website. Your rights and the response process depend on applicable law.

Changes

This notice may be updated as Jimnaya’s services and data practices change. The version published on this page applies from its publication date.
